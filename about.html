about me

Hi! ☺

I'm Nina, and I've recently completed my Bachelor of Games Development at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS)! Throughout my degree, I've been developing my skills in programming, marketing, project management, and game design. My main goal throughout my career is to make an impact, whether that's through developing engaging serious games to help solve a problem in a creative way or creating fun games for entertainment. I'm so grateful to be able to make interactive and engaging experiences for audiences to enjoy.

I love problem-solving and tackling coding problems which is why I have consistently undertaken programming roles in my projects. I have worked on a range of projects, from a 2D platformer to a 3D augmented-reality experience. I have particularly loved working on NPC behaviour trees and multiplayer systems in my university subjects and I look forward to challenging myself with a wider range of problems and games.

I have also loved and valued education throughout my life and think that teaching and learning is an essential part of being in a team. For the past couple of years I have volunteered in the Girls Programming Network to teach high-school and primary-school aged female and gender diverse students to code in Python! It's a very rewarding position to share my love of coding with people curious to learn more about it. In Winter 2026, I also participated in the week-long youth outreach trip with UTS Engineers Without Borders to teach high-school and primary-school aged students in Bateman's Bay about STEM. I helped teach a total of 19 classes throughout that week alongside 18 student volunteers and it was an extremely rewarding trip!

I have also worked on two marketing teams during my time at university. In 2026, I was part of the marketing subcommittee of Playmakers, the student games development society at UTS. In this role I helped create multiple graphics and videos. I love to learn new skills and try out different things and it was really fun to apply myself in a very different role compared to my usual programming roles. I also worked in the marketing team for Playmakers Development Team for their game Terminal Horizon. In this role I focused on editing promotional short-form content for their social media accounts, along with some graphics.

Overall, I'm looking forward to collaborating with other passionate and creative individuals to make games with a positive impact, whether that's a game made for fun, a game made to solve a problem, or a game made to tell an important story in an interactive format.
