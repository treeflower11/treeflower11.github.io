welcome to my portfolio! ☺

I'm Nina, a recent graduate Games Development graduate from the University of Technology Sydney. I have worked on a range of projects as a programmer, from a 2D platformer to a 3D augmented-reality experience designed for the Apple Vision Pro. I also have experience in teaching through volunteer programs as well as marketing through student societies and teams.

I'd like to continue to develop my skills as a games programmer to contribute to an array of systems, whether that's in-game UI or NPC behaviour trees. I'm always happy to challenge myself and learn something new!

I'm looking forward to collaborating with passionate and creative individuals to create games with positive impact, whether that's through telling important stories in an interactive format, solving real-world problems, or creating engaging and fun experiences for entertainment.
