description

Created in my subject iOS Industry Studio, my team and I worked collaboratively with our clients at the Human Augmentation Lab to create a game to test user's spatial awareness through the use of audio pings. This project was created with the intention of helping the application of spatial audio in both games and real-life scenarios for blind and visually impaired users and players.
