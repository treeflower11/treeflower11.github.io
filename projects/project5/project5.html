description

Final-year capstone project for subject Game Design Studio 1. Worked alongside 5 students to create a fast-paced LAN multiplayer party game featuring a series of minigames subdivided by different dish types.

technical details

Language: C#

Game Engine: Unity

contributions
  • Created frameworks for all major systems including the order, minigame, and sabotage systems to give group members a foundation to build their own minigames and sabotages.
  • Designed multiple minigames such as the assemble, bug-smash, fish drop, and chopping minigames.
